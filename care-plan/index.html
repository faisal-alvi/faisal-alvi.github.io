How a typical cycle works

  1. Every plugin, theme, and core update gets applied to a fresh staging copy first, never straight to your live site.
  2. I run a full regression pass: homepage, shop, cart, checkout, account, and anything gated or member-only, using a real test account where relevant.
  3. Anything that looks risky gets held back with a written reason, not silently skipped.
  4. Once staging checks out clean, the same updates go to production, and I verify there too.
  5. You get a short written summary of the cycle: what shipped, what was held, what to know.

Who this is for

Sites where an update going wrong actually costs you, orders, members, or trust. If a broken checkout or a locked-out membership area for even an hour would hurt, this is built for that. If your site is low-stakes and a weekend of downtime wouldn't matter, you probably don't need this yet.
